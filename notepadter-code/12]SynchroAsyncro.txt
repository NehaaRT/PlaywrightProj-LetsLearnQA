Almost everything in Playwright is asynchronous.
 
 
1. Synchronous (Sequential)
 
  1]Each task must finish before the next one starts. 
  2]In a synchronous system, tasks are performed one at a time. 
  3]If a task takes a long time (like waiting for a server), 
  4]the entire process is "blocked" and has to wait.
 
 --------------------------------
 example:  Real-world analogy:
 
   a]A line at a coffee shop.
   b]You order and stand in the line.
   c]Wait for the barista to make your drink, receive it,and
   d]then the next person in line can order.

 ================================================================================= 
2. Asynchronous (Parallel/Non-blocking)
 
    1]an asynchronous system, you can start a task and
    2]then move on to the next one without waiting for the first one to finish. 
    3]When the long-running task finally completes, the system notifies you (often via a "promise").
 
 -------------------------
 example : Real-world analogy:
 
     a]A line at a coffee shop.
