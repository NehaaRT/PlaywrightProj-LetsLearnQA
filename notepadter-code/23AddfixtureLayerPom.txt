Page Object Model
 
Right now, in our test file, we are instantiating the pages manually inside the tests themselves
 
For example: const loginPage = new LoginPage(page);
 
In real-world Playwright frameworks, we eliminate this boilerplate by creating Custom Fixtures.
 
This layer automatically instantiates your page objects and passes them directly into your test arguments.
 
How it improves your project:
 
Cleaner Test Files: Your tests focus entirely on the business logic, not setup mechanics.
